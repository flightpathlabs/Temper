# Changelog

## Unreleased

First version.

- Your hits appear in one column by your target's nameplate and follow the
  enemy. Choose how they leave (rise and fade, arc away, shrink away),
  which side of the nameplate they sit on, how far off, and how large.
- Critical hits are larger, land with a pop, and can be gold, red or left
  in their own colour. Spell damage can be tinted by school. Misses, dodges
  and parries show as small grey words.
- A Record button, a key binding and `/temper record` start and stop a
  recording. Fights outside a recording are kept as "Recent fights".
- An analysis page for each recording: damage a second, damage a fight,
  time in battle, how often you crit, the hardest single use of an ability,
  and a bar for each ability with its share and its damage per use.
- Damage is sorted into skills, auto attacks, pet, and items and other
  effects. Fights against one enemy and against several are told apart.
- Any two recordings can be compared. Each figure shows how far it moved,
  and the gear that differs between them is listed.
- Solo only. In a group the column steps aside and nothing is recorded.
- The game's own damage numbers are switched off while the column is
  showing and put back afterwards. This can be turned off.
